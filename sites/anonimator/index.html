Laboratory and life science

Reports, deviations and clinical texts

The work

The team wants AI to summarise study reports, analytical results, deviations and clinical texts. Those files name patients, investigators and the person who signs, and they carry dates and ID numbers.

The risk

If the file leaves as it is, the AI provider, often outside Switzerland or the EU, receives personal data and sometimes health data. The provider also receives the scientific content: removing the name does not hide the result.

The rules

  • Swiss data-protection law (revFADP / nDSG, Art. 6, 7, 8 and 16): use only what is needed, protect from the design stage and secure the system. If the text leaves the country, Anonimator is a supporting technical measure, not the permission to send it out.
  • The EU regulation (GDPR, Art. 5, 9, 25, 32 and 44 to 46): reduce the data, take care with health data and protect the transfer. It supports a transfer. It does not replace the standard contracts the law requires to send data outside Europe.
  • If the file comes from research on people, ethics approval and consent are still required (Swiss Human Research Act, LRH / HFG). If the process is GxP, Quality can include the system in revalidation: Annex 11, Annex 15 and GAMP 5.

What Anonimator does

It turns identifiers into placeholders before sending. If it cannot check the request, it sends nothing. The log keeps how many items were replaced and a fingerprint of the file, not the names. That pack can go into change control.

Fine-grained rules for health data are planned and are not included yet. The scientific text reaches the provider if the document is sent.

University and research

Teaching, real files and data not yet published

The work

Teachers, research groups and administrators use cloud AI on coursework, records, project reports and results that are not yet published.

The risk

Names of students, staff and participants leave. So does the group's knowledge: a result or method that is not yet published stays readable once the name is only a placeholder.

The rules

  • Swiss data-protection law (revFADP / nDSG) for use in Switzerland. The EU regulation (GDPR) where partners, students or participants are in the EU. Art. 44 to 46 apply if the provider sits outside the EEA: a transfer abroad needs a legal basis, and this program does not replace it.
  • The Swiss Human Research Act (LRH / HFG) and its ordinance, if the project studies people. The protocol and the ethics committee are still required. An intermediate program does not replace them.
  • Trade secrecy (Swiss Unfair Competition Act, UWG / LCD, and EU Directive 2016/943) protects unpublished information against someone who takes it without a right. It does not stop the provider from reading a text the organisation itself chooses to send.

What Anonimator does

It removes identifiers from the file before it reaches the model. The key that puts the names back does not leave the site. The person who sends the document still decides whether that unpublished text may leave.

It does not protect the intellectual property in the content. An unpublished paper, result or method stays visible to the provider if the document is sent.

Law firms, contracts and matters

Matters, contracts and correspondence

The work

A law firm or an in-house legal team wants to summarise a file, compare clauses or prepare a paper. The file holds the parties, IBANs, signatures, prices and the strategy of the matter.

The risk

The duty to keep the secret does not disappear because the tool has become common. If the whole file leaves, the provider reads who the client is and what the advice is. Changing the name covers the identity. It does not cover the clause or the price.

The rules

  • Professional secrecy: Art. 321 of the Swiss Criminal Code, and the Lawyers Act (LLCA / BGFA). The duty stays with the professional.
  • Swiss law (revFADP / nDSG) and the EU regulation (GDPR) for the people named in the contract or the file: names, contacts and identifiers.
  • Sending the text to a provider abroad can be a disclosure outside the country. Anonimator is a supporting technical measure. It is not the contract that authorises the transfer, and it is not the lawyer's decision on whether that document may leave.

What Anonimator does

It replaces parties, emails, phones and identifiers before the paper reaches the model, and puts them back in the answer the user sees. If it cannot check, it blocks the send.

It does not keep the secret of the matter. Clauses, amounts and strategy still leave if the document is sent. The duty in Art. 321 stays with the lawyer, not with the program.
