The challenge

Researchers, lab teams and administrative staff want cloud AI to summarise reports and draft documents. But those documents contain personal data: names, dates, contact details, ID numbers, health information. Sending them as they are to an external provider raises hard questions.

1

Who sees the names in our documents?

2

What about health data and data about minors?

3

Cloud AI without uploading identities?

How it works

1

Staff work as usual

Requests to ChatGPT, Claude or Copilot go through Anonimator, an OpenAI-compatible proxy on your premises.

2

Identities become placeholders

Names, emails, phones, IBAN, ID numbers, dates. If it can't check, nothing is sent.

3

Real data restored

The cloud AI answers with placeholders; Anonimator puts the real data back for your user.

Key benefits

1

Personal data detection

Names, emails, phones, IBAN, AHV/AVS, DNI/NIE, dates, licence plates.

2

Fail-closed by default

If Anonimator can't check a request, nothing is sent.

3

Re-identification under your control

Keys and mappings are managed in your infrastructure.

4

Purpose-aware policies

Strict handling of health data and data about minors.

5

Audit without personal data

Oversight without creating new records of identities.

6

Your formats, five languages

Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, text; OCR optional. ES, DE, FR, IT, EN.
