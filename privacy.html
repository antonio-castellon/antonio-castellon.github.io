Privacy Statement for Castellon.CH Desktop Apps

Effective date: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Statement applies to the desktop apps GxP Sonar and AgentDesk (“the Apps”), published by Castellon.CH, Antonio Castellon, Biel/Bienne, Switzerland (“we” or “us”).

In short: the Apps sign you in with your own Microsoft account and call Copilot Studio on your behalf. We run no server that stores your credentials. Your sign-in tokens stay on your device.

1. Sign-in with Microsoft

The Apps sign you in with your Microsoft account through Microsoft Entra ID. Sign-in happens on Microsoft's own sign-in page or the sign-in component of your operating system. The Apps never ask you to type your password into a form of their own, and we never see your password.

2. What the Apps do with your account

After you sign in, the Apps use the delegated permission you consent to (Power Platform API, CopilotStudio.Copilots.Invoke) to call Copilot Studio on your behalf. These requests go directly from your device to Microsoft. That processing is governed by Microsoft's terms and privacy statement and by your organisation's settings: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement.

3. Where your tokens are kept

The access and refresh tokens issued by Microsoft are stored only on your device, in your operating system's credential store. They are not sent to us.

4. What we do not do

  • We run no server that stores your credentials or tokens.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

5. Applicable law and your rights

We handle personal data in line with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You can ask us about any personal data concerning you at the contact address below.

6. Changes to this statement

We will update this statement when the Apps' data handling changes and revise the effective date above.
