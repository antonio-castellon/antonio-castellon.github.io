ANONIMATOR

Use cloud AI on real documents,
without sending identities.

A local gateway between your people and cloud AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot). It replaces personal data with placeholders before anything leaves your premises, then restores it in the answer.

The external AI provider receives no identifying data; the key never leaves your premises.

What it is for

Your teams want cloud AI to summarise reports, review files and draft documents. But lab, study, research, teaching and administrative documents contain personal data: names, dates, contact details, AHV/AVS numbers, health information. Sending them to an external AI provider as they are is a risk.

Anonimator lets people keep working with the AI tools they already use. Personal data is pseudonymised on your own infrastructure before a request leaves, and the real data is put back into the answer when it returns.

Who sees the names?

Identities in your documents stay on your premises; the cloud AI only sees placeholders.

Health data and minors

Purpose-aware policies give strict handling of health data and data about minors.

Cloud AI, no identities

Same AI capabilities, with no identities shared with the AI provider.

How it works

Your people → Anonimator (on your premises) → cloud AI


1. Work as usual

Requests to ChatGPT, Claude or Copilot go through Anonimator, an OpenAI-compatible proxy on your premises.

2. Identities become placeholders

Names, emails, phone numbers, IBAN, AHV/AVS, DNI/NIE, dates and plates are replaced: Anna Meier → [PERSON_7f3a]. If Anonimator can't check, nothing is sent.

3. Real data restored

The cloud AI answers with placeholders; Anonimator puts the real data back. The key is then destroyed.

Key benefits

  • On-premise, standard CPUs: no GPU needed. Works as an OpenAI-compatible proxy.
  • The key stays with you: mapping only in memory, on premises, destroyed after each query.
  • Fail-closed by design: if it can't check, nothing is sent.
  • Purpose-aware policies: strict handling of health data and data about minors.
  • Audit without personal data: oversight without creating new records of identities.
  • Your formats, five languages: Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, text (ODF, CSV, RTF, HTML, email, OCR optional). ES, DE, FR, IT, EN.
