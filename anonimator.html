ANONIMATOR

Use ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot
without sending identifying data.

Anonimator pseudonymises names, emails, IBANs, national IDs and other personal data on your own premises before a prompt or document reaches an external AI provider, and restores the original values in the answer.

The external provider receives no identifying data; the key never leaves your premises.

Data protection and AI rules

Designed to support data minimisation, pseudonymisation and privacy by design under the GDPR (Art. 4(5), 5, 25, 32) and the Swiss revFADP (Art. 6–8), and responsible use of AI under the EU AI Act.

EU

GDPR

Art. 4(5), 5, 8, 9, 25, 30, 32, 44–46

Pseudonymisation, data minimisation, privacy by design and security of processing, with strict rules for special categories and children, and PII-free accountability evidence.

Art. 6, 7, 8, 16

Proportionality, privacy by design and by default, data security, and a supporting measure for cross-border disclosure. Runs entirely on Swiss/customer premises.

Art. 4; Art. 10 only for high-risk systems

Central policy for what data may reach external AI tools, enforced in the browser and API, with PII-free audit evidence.

How it works

Your people→Anonimator (on your premises)→Cloud AI
1. WORK AS USUAL

Same tools

Requests to ChatGPT, Claude or Copilot go through Anonimator, an OpenAI-compatible proxy on your premises.

2. PLACEHOLDERS

Identities are replaced

Names, emails, phone numbers, IBAN, AHV/AVS, DNI/NIE, dates and plates: Anna Meier → [PERSON_7f3a]. If Anonimator can't check, nothing is sent.

3. RESTORED

Real data back in the answer

The cloud AI answers with placeholders; Anonimator puts the real data back. The key is then destroyed.

Technical details (for IT teams)
  • On-premise, standard CPUs, no GPU needed. OpenAI-compatible proxy.
  • Per-query, RAM-only mapping, destroyed after each query.
  • Fail-closed leak check before egress.
  • Audit trail with counts and hashes only, hash-chained.
