MCP-GLP-AUDIT-PROXY

Your AI agents touch lab systems.
Who keeps the audit trail?

AI agents can now read and write in the tools your lab already owns. Without a record of who did what, when, and through which AI tool, that is a data-integrity gap. mcp-glp-audit-proxy is the control layer between the AI brain and your tools: every tool call is checked, optionally approved, and logged in a tamper-evident chain.

How it works

AI brain → control layer → tools you already own


1. Intercept

The proxy sits between your MCP client (the AI agent) and your MCP servers. Every tool call passes through it.

2. Control

An allow/denylist decides what may run. Write operations can require human approval (glp_approve). If something is wrong, it fails closed: the call is blocked, not let through.

3. Record & verify

Each call is appended to a JSONL hash chain (RFC 8785 canonical JSON + SHA-256). The verify CLI detects any tampering, and --print-head lets you anchor the chain head externally.
