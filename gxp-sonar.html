GXP SONAR

Your GxP Excel sheets,
reviewed with nothing to install.

GxP Sonar is a portable Windows app that reviews folders of Excel workbooks and flags GxP data-integrity risks: formulas, VBA macros, protection, external links and hand-typed values where a formula should be. No admin rights, no installation: it runs with the employee's own permissions.

Read-only by default: GxP Sonar does not modify, save or run your files. Writing in the analysed folders is a separate, optional add-on.

The challenge

Labs and quality teams rely on hundreds of Excel sheets full of calculations, macros and links. Reviewing them by hand for an audit or a validation takes days, and errors in a single cell or macro go unnoticed.

When to use it: before an audit, a validation or a migration.

01

Portable, no admin

A single .exe, no installation or services; uses the employee's permissions.

02

Read-only by default

Does not save your files, run macros or refresh links. Writing is a separate, optional add-on: it lets Codex, Claude, Copilot and Grok write in the analysed folders and must be requested and included in the licence.

03

Exact location

Every finding with its risk, sheet and cell, or VBA module and line.

04

Your own AI, no extra licences

Grok Build, Codex, Claude, Gemini, Copilot Studio or an OpenAI-compatible service, on your own account.

05

You decide what the AI sees

Structure only, masked data or full data (the last one only with your consent).

06

PDF reports

A draft to review, in the language the analysis is run in: each file with its SHA-256 hash, app version and build date.

How it works

Your Excel folders→GxP Sonar (on your PC, read-only by default)→Findings and PDF report to review
1. PICK A FOLDER

Nothing to install

Open GxPSonar.exe and pick the folder. Only what you can already open is analysed.

2. LOCAL ANALYSIS

No AI (phase 1)

Reviews each workbook on your PC and records every finding with its risk and exact location: sheet and cell, or VBA module and line. Nothing leaves the machine.

3. AI ANALYSIS (PRO)

Optional

With your own AI engine, it explains the findings and compares the files with each other, in plain language with precise GxP terms.

AI data levels (Pro)

The AI runs on the customer's own account; GxP Sonar adds no AI licences. Without AI, the analysis stays on your PC.

Please note: Any local CLI engine acts with its own permissions and can modify files (e.g. after a malicious prompt); its security depends on that provider and its configuration.

Built-in GxP prompts

Seven built-in prompts for the AI analysis. Each one cites the clause it refers to, for guidance. They produce draft evidence for a person to review; they do not decide whether a sheet is fit for use.

Please note

GxP Sonar helps the owner of the files get ready before any audit. The AI can be wrong, so the review and the decision always stay with a person. It does not replace validation or the judgement of the responsible person.
