Privacy Policy for Oigo

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Oigo (“we”, “us”, or “the App”) handles information. Oigo is an accessibility interface that connects to Grok to help blind and visually impaired users interact with the service through voice or other accessible means.

We do not collect, store, or process any personal or device data from users of Oigo.

1. Information We Collect

Oigo does not collect any personal information, usage data, device identifiers, location data, or any other user or device information.
The App acts only as an interface to Grok. Any interaction with Grok is handled directly by Grok’s own services and is subject to Grok’s privacy policy.

2. How We Use Information

Because we collect no user data, we do not use any personal information for any purpose.

3. How We Share Information

We do not collect any information and therefore do not share any user data with third parties.

4. Data Retention

No user data is retained by Oigo.

5. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data for you to access, correct, or delete through the App.
If you have questions about data processed by Grok itself, please refer to Grok’s privacy policy.

6. Children’s Privacy

Oigo is not directed at children and does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

7. Security

No user data is collected or stored by the App.

8. International Transfers

No user data is transferred by Oigo.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App’s functionality changes. The “Last updated” date will be revised. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
